WHY QUIXOTE

You already have the time. It’s just stuck in traffic.

Learning a language takes hundreds of hours, and most of us never find them. But a 30-minute drive each way adds up to five hours a week, every week, already in your calendar. Quixote turns that time into practice: a coach talks, you answer out loud, and the app helps you get it right. No screen, no new habit to build.

It’s built for the car: short lessons that pick up where you left off, with no need to look at your phone once you press start.
