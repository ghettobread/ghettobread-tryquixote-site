Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Quixote is a hands-free language app for your commute. It’s in beta, and this page explains in plain words what it collects and why. Short version: no account, no email, no recordings of your voice. If you share usage (on by default, easy to turn off), the app sends how you use it and the text of your spoken answers, under a random ID.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, scores, settings, the name or nickname you choose, and anything you tell the characters about yourself in story episodes are saved on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app sends, if you share usage

  • A random tester ID created when you install the app. It isn’t linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.
  • How you use the app: lessons started and finished, how far you got, scores, which screens and buttons you used, voice commands, and microphone or audio playback problems (for example, a line that cut out).
  • The text of your spoken answers (up to 120 characters each), as your phone recognized them, along with the phrase you were practising. This is how we find phrases people struggle with. If you answer a question about yourself in a story episode (where you’re from, for example), that answer’s text is included.
  • Your device: phone model, iOS version, app version, which language you’re learning, and a tester code if you entered one.
  • Approximate location (city, region, country), estimated from your internet connection by our analytics provider. We don’t use GPS.
  • Feedback you send from the app, including voice notes: if you say “note” during a lesson, what you say next is sent as text, as your phone recognized it, with the lesson and step you were on. Your voice itself is never recorded or sent. Feedback is sent even if usage sharing is off, because sending it is your choice each time.
  • Your name or nickname, only if the app doesn’t already have it recorded, so we can record a voice clip of it for the lessons.

What we don’t collect

We never record, store, or upload your voice. We don’t collect your email (unless you join the launch list, below), contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers, and there are no ads.

Speech recognition

To understand your answers, Quixote uses Apple’s built-in speech recognition. Depending on your iPhone and language, Apple may process the audio on its servers. That’s covered by Apple’s privacy policy, and Apple doesn’t share the audio with us. We only receive the recognized text described above.

The launch list

If you join the launch list or invite a friend, you fill in a short form hosted by Tally, which asks for your email and which languages you want to learn. The form also receives your tester ID, so referral rewards go to the right people. We use your email only to tell you when Quixote launches and to give you your reward. Your language choices help us decide which languages to build next.

Visiting tryquixote.com

When you visit this website, we count the visit: the page you opened, the site that sent you there (and any campaign tag in the link), your browser and device type, approximate location estimated from your internet connection, and whether you tapped the beta, TestFlight, or launch-list links. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, so visits aren’t linked to each other or to you, and they aren’t linked to the app.

Who helps us run Quixote

  • PostHog (analytics, United States) receives the usage data above and the website visit counts.
  • Tally hosts the launch-list form.
  • Apple provides speech recognition, TestFlight, and the App Store.
  • Expo delivers app updates. Like any download, it sees your internet address.
  • ElevenLabs records the voice clips. The only thing of yours it ever receives is a name or nickname we record a clip for.

We don’t sell your data or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Usage data is kept while Quixote is in beta and development, to improve the lessons. Launch-list emails are kept until launch rewards are given out or you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

  • Turn off sharing: in the app, open Progress and settings and switch off Share usage. Nothing more is sent, and anything waiting to be sent is cleared.
  • Delete your data: email us. Include your tester code if you set one, or the email you used for the launch list. Because the app has no accounts, we may not be able to find usage data that can’t be matched to you.
  • Leave the launch list at any time by emailing us.

Children

Quixote isn’t made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has used it, email us and we’ll delete what we can find.

Changes

If what the app or website collects changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
