Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Quixote is a hands-free language app for your commute. It’s in beta, and this page explains in plain words what it collects and why. Short version: no recordings of your voice, ever. You can use the app without an account. If you share usage (on by default, easy to turn off), the app sends how you use it and the text of your spoken answers, under a random ID. If you make a profile, we store it so it follows you to a new phone, and it’s public unless you set it to private.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, scores, settings, the name or nickname you choose, how you usually get around, and anything you tell the characters about yourself in story episodes are saved on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them (unless you have a profile, below).

Distance learned is an estimate the app works out on your phone, from your learning time and how you said you usually get around. The app doesn’t read your location for it.

What the app sends, if you share usage

  • A random tester ID created when you install the app. It isn’t linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.
  • How you use the app: lessons started and finished, how far you got, scores, which screens and buttons you used, voice commands, and microphone or audio playback problems (for example, a line that cut out).
  • The text of your spoken answers (up to 120 characters each), as your phone recognized them, along with the phrase you were practising. This is how we find phrases people struggle with. If you answer a question about yourself in a story episode (where you’re from, for example), that answer’s text is included.
  • Your device: phone model, iOS version, app version, which language you’re learning, and a tester code if you entered one.
  • Approximate location (city, region, country), estimated from your internet connection by our analytics provider. We don’t use GPS.
  • Feedback you send from the app, including voice notes: if you say “note” during a lesson, what you say next is sent as text, as your phone recognized it, with the lesson and step you were on. Your voice itself is never recorded or sent. Feedback is sent even if usage sharing is off, because sending it is your choice each time.

If you make a profile

Profiles are optional. You sign in with Apple or Google, and we store your profile with Supabase so it’s backed up and follows you to a new phone.

  • From your sign-in: an account ID and your email address (with Sign in with Apple, you can hide your email, and Apple gives us a private relay address instead). We use your email only for your account and launch rewards.
  • What you choose to add: your name, a username, a picture from our set of avatars (you can’t upload photos yet), up to two flags for where you or your family are from, and the colors of your page. All of these are optional except the username.
  • Your progress: your belt, learning time, lessons finished, scores, languages, and the distance estimate, so they can be restored on a new phone. We also keep where your learning time and distance stood at the start of each week, for group leaderboards.
  • Your invite link, so referral rewards go to the right people.

Public or private. Profiles start public: anyone with the link to your page (tryquixote.com/u/your-username) can see your name, username, avatar, flags, belt, learning time, distance estimate, and languages. Your page never shows where or when you travel, your answers, or your scores. You can set your profile to private at any time in Settings: then only you can see it, nobody can look up your username, and your invite link still works.

Groups. Groups are optional and private: up to six people, joined only with the group’s code. If you join one, its members see your name, avatar, belt, learning time and distance (this week and all time), even if your profile is private. We store the group’s name, code, icon and theme, who is in it, and the icon and theme picked by that week’s top learner. Groups use icons from our set; you can’t upload photos to them yet. Leave a group at any time; a group is deleted when its last member leaves, and deleting your account removes you from every group.

Reporting. If you see a profile with offensive content, use the Report link on the page or email us, and we’ll review it and remove what breaks our rules.

What we don’t collect

We never record, store, or upload your voice. We don’t collect your email (unless you make a profile or join the launch list), contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers, and there are no ads.

Speech recognition

To understand your answers, Quixote uses Apple’s built-in speech recognition. Depending on your iPhone and language, Apple may process the audio on its servers. That’s covered by Apple’s privacy policy, and Apple doesn’t share the audio with us. We only receive the recognized text described above.

The launch list

If you join the launch list or invite a friend, you fill in a short form hosted by Tally, which asks for your email and which languages you want to learn. The form also receives your tester ID, so referral rewards go to the right people. We use your email only to tell you when Quixote launches and to give you your reward. Your language choices help us decide which languages to build next.

Visiting tryquixote.com

When you visit this website, we count the visit: the page you opened, the site that sent you there (and any campaign tag in the link), your browser and device type, approximate location estimated from your internet connection, and whether you tapped the beta, TestFlight, or launch-list links. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, so visits aren’t linked to each other or to you, and they aren’t linked to the app.

Who helps us run Quixote

  • PostHog (analytics, United States) receives the usage data above and the website visit counts.
  • Supabase (database and sign-in) stores profiles, if you make one.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google, if you choose it.
  • Tally hosts the launch-list form.
  • Apple provides speech recognition, Sign in with Apple, TestFlight, and the App Store.
  • Expo delivers app updates. Like any download, it sees your internet address.
  • ElevenLabs records the voice clips for the lessons. It receives nothing of yours.

We don’t sell your data or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Usage data is kept while Quixote is in beta and development, to improve the lessons. Profiles are kept until you delete your account. Launch-list emails are kept until launch rewards are given out or you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

  • Turn off sharing: in the app, open Progress and settings and switch off Share usage. Nothing more is sent, and anything waiting to be sent is cleared.
  • Make your profile private in Settings, at any time.
  • Leave a group from the group’s menu, at any time.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile, then Delete account and data). This deletes your profile and synced progress.
  • Delete your other data: email us. Include your tester code if you set one, or the email you used for the launch list. We may not be able to find usage data that can’t be matched to you.
  • Leave the launch list at any time by emailing us.

Children

Quixote isn’t made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has used it, email us and we’ll delete what we can find.

Changes

If what the app or website collects changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
